# 0001 Scope and deliverables
Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-03

Context: ExaCare's Toronto Product Manager posting says PMs "create prototypes". A concrete piece of product thinking shows more than a description of it.
Decision: Build three things in ExaCare's post-acute care space: a problem statement, a short pitch deck, and a proof of concept.
Alternatives: A written analysis only (rejected: less concrete, and nothing to run).
Consequences: The problem statement and deck are the core deliverable; the POC is a stretch goal and must fit the timebox (ADR 0002).
