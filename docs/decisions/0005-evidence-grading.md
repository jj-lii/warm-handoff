# 0005 Evidence grading and citation rule
Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-03

Context: Company-published material is self-reported by nature. The project favors credible, scientific outside sources.
Decision: Tag every source with a tier: (1) peer-reviewed or government (CMS, OIG, MedPAC, AHRQ), (2) trade or industry body, (3) news, (4) company-reported. ExaCare's own statistics (99% accuracy, time-to-accept benchmarks, facility counts) are tier 4 and are never presented as established fact. Every number in a deliverable needs a cited source with link, year and sample or method where given; unsourced numbers are cut. If strong sources contradict an ExaCare premise, say so.
Alternatives: Treat ExaCare's published figures as givens (rejected: they're self-reported).
Consequences: More reading per claim. Some persuasive points will be dropped for lack of a source.
